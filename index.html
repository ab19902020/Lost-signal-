THE SHELTER

The walk-in armoury has a working security door, an animated quartermaster and complete weapon racks. Open the door, then USE any rifle, shotgun, sniper, submachine gun, pistol, revolver or blade to take it down — all twenty-six fire, reload and sound like themselves, and the one you put down goes back on its hook. Hold FIRE for the automatics. The CCTV console watches the surface compound on four feeds, and the silo's top landing on a fifth.

The secure gallery at the top of Silo 47 has a sentry on its door, a working dog on the ring, and an infirmary that will treat you three times before its stores run out.

The surface runs a clock. Sun, moon, stars and weather all move: the compound lights come on at dusk and go off at dawn, and it rains when the cloud comes in. The shooting range down the west side has six falling plates from 13 to 30 metres — take a weapon out of the armoury and find out what it does. The town on the south-west horizon is where this goes next.

The hatch in the floor opens onto the silo's top landing. The stair spirals down seven galleries to the stores at the bottom; the residents on the way will talk to you. Mind the light well — it is a long way down.
