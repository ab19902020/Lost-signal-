THE SHELTER

The walk-in armoury has a working security door, an animated quartermaster and complete weapon racks. Enter it and use the marked service rifle to equip it. The CCTV console watches the surface compound on four feeds, and the silo's top landing on a fifth.

The hatch in the floor opens onto the silo's top landing. The stair spirals down seven galleries to the stores at the bottom; the residents on the way will talk to you. Mind the light well — it is a long way down.
